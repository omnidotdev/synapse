Serve the card-verified free AI allowance on each product's free model

A free account whose owner verified a card (Aether funding `cardVerified`) may now spend its wallet on chat, held to the product's `free_model` (Haiku 5.5 for Keystone, Facet, Beacon, and Creator) whatever model it asks for. Images, speech, embeddings, cascades, and products without a `free_model` stay paid-only, and an account without a verified card is refused exactly as before.
